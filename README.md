# BrainNest Academy | Business Process Analysis & Data-Driven Decision-Making

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business process analysis and data-driven decision-making case study for **BrainNest Academy**, a higher education institution in Sri Lanka.

The study examines how organizational data supports operational efficiency, student engagement, marketing, and strategic decisions. It also explores the application of business intelligence tools, workflow modelling, and data analysis to address institutional challenges.

## Business Problem

BrainNest Academy faces challenges in expanding awareness and enrolment, particularly among underserved Tamil-speaking communities.

The project focuses on understanding enrolment trends, student demographics, language preferences, and outreach effectiveness to identify opportunities for improving recruitment and strategic decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse existing organizational processes and information flows.
- Identify operational and decision-making challenges.
- Evaluate the role of organizational data in improving business performance.
- Examine data security, privacy, and ethical considerations.
- Design a data-driven solution using structured workflows.
- Explore and apply Excel and Power BI for business analysis.
- Develop recommendations to support management decisions.

## Project Methodology

**1. Business Process Analysis**

Examined academic administration, student admissions, marketing, financial management, student engagement, and operational processes.

**2. Data Management and Governance**

Analysed data collection, processing, information security, ethical considerations, and risk mitigation.

**3. Data Science and Business Intelligence**

Evaluated analytical tools and techniques for supporting business requirements and improving decision-making.

**4. Solution Design and Workflow Modelling**

Developed a structured workflow covering the proposed analytical solution, its processes, data sources, and implementation considerations.

**5. Data Analysis and Visualization**

Explored the use of Microsoft Excel and Power BI to analyse business information, visualize findings, and support recommendations.

**6. Strategic Recommendations**

Proposed improvements to marketing, student outreach, and organizational decision-making based on the analysis.
